1 Introduction054
A model that answers benchmark questions cor- 055
rectly may be doing one of two empirically sep- 056
arable things. It may be following the problem’s 057
structure to arrive at an answer that survives surface 058
perturbation. Or it may be retrieving a familiar an- 059
swer from training data, in which case its accuracy 060
depends on the proximity between the test items 061
and its training distribution. Standard accuracy re- 062
porting collapses both regimes into one number, 063
treating models that look identical on a leaderboard 064
as if they were the same kind of system.065
The accuracy–robustness divergence.This mat- 066
ters concretely. On adversarial shortest-path plan- 067
ning, Claude Sonnet 4 achieves 64.7% canonical 068
accuracy—higher than GPT-4o’s 41.2%. When 069
city labels are renamed (variant W3) while pre- 070
serving graph structure exactly, Claude drops to 071
0.0% and GPT-4o retains 26.5%. The model with 072
higher benchmark accuracy is the model with lower 073
out-of-distribution robustness. On adversarial coin- 074
change the inversion reverses: Claude retains 60% 075
W3 accuracy, GPT-4o drops to 0%. The diver- 076
gence is subtype-specific—an interaction between 077
each model’s training distribution and the subtype’s 078
training-corpus proximity, not a model-level trait.079
Why a single probe is not enough.A drop under 080
entity rename is provocative but not diagnostic. It 081
could reflect a harder input, a tokenization artifact, 082
or a distributional shift unrelated to retrieval. Any 083
single probe can be explained away. Our solution 084

is convergence: independent probes that point to085
the same per-instance signal.086
Behavioral Triangulation.We deploy three or-087
thogonal probes on every problem instance (Fig-088
ure 1):089
Probe 1 — Surface invariance. Six structure-090
preserving variants W1–W6; the diagnostic091
is the gap between canonical accuracy and092
entity-rename (W3) accuracy.093
Probe 2 — Plan–execution coupling. Phase 1:094
the model declares a complete strategy. Phase095
2A: it executes step-by-step in a fresh session.096
Phase 2B: at a uniformly sampled step we inject097
a false intermediate state and observe both098
compliance and recovery.099
Probe 3 — Training-data proximity. N-gram100
overlap with The Pile and DCLM via the101
Infini-gram index (?).102
None of these is novel in isolation. Surface-103
sensitivity studies (???) report aggregate accuracy104
drops. Chain-of-thought faithfulness work (??)105
shows stated reasoning can misrepresent the un-106

ses (??) regress accuracy on n-gram statistics. Our108
contribution is theper-instance alignmentof all109
three: a problem on which one model fails entity re-110
name, also fails plan-execution coupling, and also111

Why this family of three.The three probes114
are chosen to be orthogonal under retrieval115
and co-active under computation. A retrieval-116
driven process is surface-sensitive (low RW3 ),117
plan-decoupled (verbal plan as artifact), and118
contamination-driven. A structure-sensitive pro-119
cess is surface-invariant, plan-coupled, and120
contamination-independent. The three probes are121
the dimensions on which the two regimes empiri-122
cally separate in our pilots; we do not claim they123
exhaust all possible diagnostic axes (see Limita-124
tions).125

6 Discussion557
Accuracy is not sufficient for capability claims.558
A model with higher benchmark accuracy can be559
the model with lower structure-preserving robust-560
ness, and the inversion direction depends on the561
subtype. Reporting canonical accuracy alone mea-562
sures retrieval fidelity, not generalization.563
Two empirically separable processing regimes.564
Claude and GPT-4o reach identical GSM canon-565
ical accuracy by different routes. Claude exe-566
cutes rigidly (CCI 0.231, CCI–correctness r = 0.14567
within-subject): following the plan does not predict568
correctness. GPT-4o reaches correct answers with569
median CCI 0. The injection-compliance asymme-570
try adds a layer: Claude and GPT-4o both accept571
injected false states in >88% of cases but recover572
the final answer ∼50% of the time; Gemini ignores573
the injection entirely. These are distinct behavioral574
signatures invisible to a single accuracy column.575
Algorithm-invocation as surface behavior.576
That 13 of 13 algorithm-invocation steps yielded577
incorrect final answers, distributed across three578
of four models, on problems no harder than the579
rest of the ALGO set, suggests that algorithm-580
naming in step-by-step traces is a learned surface581
behavior associated with optimization-problem582
contexts. The Wilson upper bound (22.8%)583
and the non-significant Fisher comparison ( p =584
0.40) mean we cannot elevate this to a strong585
statistical claim from the current observational586
sample, but the qualitative pattern is consistent587
across models. The methodological implication588
is that chain-of-thought traces saying “I will use589
algorithm X” should not be treated as evidence the590
model is executing X. This complements?and?591
on CoT unfaithfulness: they show stated reasoning592
can misrepresent causal factors; we observe that593
verbal algorithm-naming specifically does not594
correspond to executing the named algorithm.595
WIS as a natural contamination control.596
Weighted interval scheduling provides a cleaner597
test than any constructed OOD dataset. Zero tem-598
plate contamination by direct measurement, struc-599
turally identical to coin-change (both DP-solvable).600
Every model collapses on WIS W3 while CC re-601
tains positive W3 for some models—consistent602
with contamination-driven retrieval and ruling out603
an explanation in which W3 is hard for purely al-604
gorithmic reasons.605
Practical guidance.For an evaluator with a lim- 606
ited budget, in priority order: (1) report canoni- 607
cal accuracy alongside W3 retention as a standard 608
pair—this exposes the inversion at the cost of one 609
additional sweep; (2) for benchmarks with subtype 610
variation, add the within-family contamination gra- 611

subset—partial CCI coverage on n = 30 problems 614

injection-compliance probe on adversarial subsets. 616
Each subsequent probe adds independent diagnos- 617
tic value.618

7 Conclusion669
A model with higher benchmark accuracy is670
not necessarily a model with stronger reasoning.671
Across 219 problems, six surface variants, four672
models, and >4,100 inference calls, three orthogo-673
nal probes converge to expose what accuracy hides.674
Two models with identical canonical GSM accu-675
racy differ by 2.1× in plan–execution coupling.676
Thirteen algorithm-invocation steps across three677
of four models produce zero correct answers, on678
problems no harder than the rest of our ALGO679
set. A zero-contamination algorithmic subtype680
collapses to zero W3 accuracy while its high-681
contamination structural twin retains positive ro-682
bustness. A reasoning-trained model retains 95.7%683
accuracy under entity rename on arithmetic, vs.684
58.3% for the strongest non-reasoning model. The685
right next step for evaluation is to report accuracy686
alongside structure-preserving perturbation results687
as a standard pair, with confidence intervals and688
