# BX-00 — The Deep Logic: Why the Sciences of Hidden Minds Are the Source

## The real objective, stated plainly
We cannot open a language model and read whether it reasoned or recalled. Correct output does not prove correct process. This is not a new problem. It is THE problem that neuroscience, experimental psychology, psychometrics, and developmental psychology each spent a century solving for the human mind — another system whose output you can see but whose process you cannot. Those fields did not just invent tasks; they invented a logic of inference for exactly this situation. The four strategies we had (validate, grade, open, train-laws) are good but shallow because they stay inside the ML paradigm. The deep, novel strategies come from importing that inferential logic.

## The field's blind spot (verified 2026-07-07)
"Machine psychology" (Hagendorff et al.) and "LLM Psychometrics" (Ye et al. systematic review, arXiv:2505.08245) already exist and are growing. But look at WHAT they borrowed: personality inventories (Big Five, HEXACO), Theory-of-Mind batteries, the Cognitive Reflection Test, value scales. They borrowed psychology's QUESTIONNAIRES. Almost nobody borrowed the METHODOLOGICAL machinery that neuropsychology built to infer hidden architecture: dissociation logic, transfer taxonomies, savings, priming, source monitoring, adaptation/habituation, developmental order-of-acquisition, individual-differences factor structure. That machinery is designed for our exact inference problem and is sitting unused for LLMs. THIS is the moat.

## The six families of inference (each = a note in this folder)
1. [[BX-01_Dissociation_Logic]] — prove two abilities are separate systems by making one break while the other survives. The single most powerful tool neuropsychology has.
2. [[BX-02_Transfer_and_Concept_Tests]] — the developmental-psychology arsenal for "does it have the concept": conservation, near/far transfer, counterfactual, reversibility, appearance-reality.
3. [[BX-03_Memory_Science_Probes]] — savings, priming, interference, source monitoring, recognition-vs-recall. Reads the SIGNATURE of memory, not just its presence.
4. [[BX-04_Adaptation_and_Habituation]] — the perceptual-neuroscience trick of repeating until response drops, to reveal what the system treats as "the same."
5. [[BX-05_Developmental_and_Lesion]] — order of acquisition over training (developmental) and ablation (lesion) to map when and where an ability lives.
6. [[BX-06_Individual_Differences_Psychometrics]] — factor analysis and IRT across a population to discover the latent dimensions of ability, not just average scores.
