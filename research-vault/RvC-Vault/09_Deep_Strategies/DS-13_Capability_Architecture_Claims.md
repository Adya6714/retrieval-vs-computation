# DS-13 — From Protocol Novelty to Capability and Architecture Claims
family: program strategy · answers the critique: "novelty is combinatorial, not a capability/architecture claim"

**The ladder.** Instrument → phenomenon → capability claim → mechanism/architecture claim → intervention → training law. The triangulation protocol is rung 1–2. Everything below is how to climb. Rule: never manufacture an architecture claim directly; reviewers reject bolted-on novelty. Earn it through the ladder.

## Capability claims within reach (ranked by distance from current data)
**CC-1. The capability space is at least two-dimensional.** Accuracy and surface-invariance are dissociable capabilities with different causal antecedents. Evidence already in hand: o4-mini WIS 1.00→0.00 under rename; Claude vs o4-mini GSM retention divergence at matched canonical accuracy (.892 vs 1.000 at .841). Needed to claim it: breadth across models/families + DS-01 measurement model + a causal antecedent (DS-04 supplies "diversity, not dose"). This is a claim about what capabilities ARE, not about a protocol.
**CC-2. Absence claim: models lack source monitoring.** After D1-lite, ask the model whether it has seen each item; if d′≈0 against ground truth, the claim is "LLMs have no privileged access to their own memorization" — a crisp negative capability result with safety consequences ([[DS-05_Machine_Source_Monitoring]]).
**CC-3. Developmental order claim.** Surface-invariance emerges after task accuracy with a measurable token lag, gradually or as a phase change ([[D04_Developmental_Checkpoints]]). A claim about how capabilities form.
**CC-4. Capability boundary claim.** A quantifiable compositional depth k beyond which retrieval can no longer substitute for computation ([[DS-09_Compositional_Stress]]).

## Architecture claims within reach
The bridge is [[P35_Emergent_Symbolic_Mechanisms_2025]] (ICML 2025): Llama models contain an emergent three-stage symbolic architecture — symbol abstraction heads → symbolic induction heads → retrieval heads — identified by causal mediation, code public.
**AC-1. Pathway-occupancy claim (do first).** Hypothesis H6: per-item solution strategy = which pathway carries the answer. Retrieval-consistent items bypass stage 1 (abstraction heads silent or non-causal; answer carried by direct token-level association); computation-consistent items show causal abstraction-head engagement. W3 collapse = items where renaming breaks a pathway that was never abstract to begin with. Test: reuse their CMA pipeline on OUR canonical/W3 pairs; measure per-item causal mediation of the identified head sets; correlate occupancy with DS-01 class posteriors, intrusion (DS-02), CD (D08). Deliverable claim: "strategy is an architectural variable — measurable circuit occupancy — and fragility is pathway bypass." That is an architecture claim about how reasoning is organized, not a protocol.
**AC-2. Repair/installation claim.** If AC-1 localizes the failure at stage 1: intervene — patch or steer abstraction-head outputs on W3 runs; fine-tune ONLY those heads (or an adapter constrained to that layer band) with a canonical↔W3 contrastive objective; show invariance is installed without harming canonical accuracy or unrelated tasks. Claim: "the invariance capability can be installed by repairing stage 1 of the symbolic architecture." Scoop-check steering-for-invariance before drafting (queue entry added).
**AC-3. Training-architecture law (the unification).** Hypothesis H7: exposure DIVERSITY (DS-04) works because varied surface forms are the training pressure that forces abstraction heads to form; verbatim repetition lets token-level shortcuts win. Test inside DS-04 arms: track abstraction-head emergence (their CMA scores) across training arms. If diversity-arm models grow stronger stage-1 circuitry, the behavioral law gets a mechanistic explanation — capability, architecture, and data law in one arc.
**AC-4 (moonshot, only after AC-1/2).** A binding-bottleneck module (relational cross-attention / Abstractors lineage cited in P35) added to a small LM to confer data-efficient invariance. Highest risk; do not lead with it.
