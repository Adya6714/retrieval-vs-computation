# DS-06 — Fragility Laws: From Catalog to Prediction
family: deeper theory · cost: analysis-only on D3 outputs

**Idea.** Fit functional forms to D3 retention-vs-distance curves (logistic, exponential, segmented). Test: (1) are per-model parameters (cliff location c, slope k) stable traits across families and reruns (reliability of the fingerprint)? (2) do parameters relate lawfully to observables — log-exposure, model scale, family? (3) the hard test: PREDICT — hold out items, predict their retention from fitted laws; then hold out a whole model, predict its curve from family/scale/exposure profile before measuring, and report predictive error honestly.

**Why deeper.** Prediction is the demarcation line between a catalog and a science. Even a failed strong prediction is a finding (fragility is idiosyncratic ⇒ per-model audits are mandatory, no shortcuts — which itself matters for [[D10_Structural_Audit_Suite]]).

**Prerequisite.** D3 ladders (HP-10) must exist. Pre-register candidate functional forms and the out-of-sample protocol before fitting anything.
