# DS-12 — Grand Synthesis: What the Program Would Establish

Not a paper. The thesis the whole vault is organized to support, stated so every smaller result can be checked against it.

**Claim.** Benchmark scores conflate two mechanisms — retrieval of exposed patterns and fresh computation — that (1) can be separated per instance by a validated multi-indicator measurement model (DS-01/02/03), (2) are calibrated against controlled and ecological exposure ground truth (D1/D4), (3) obey predictable laws relating surface distance, compositional depth, and exposure variance to fragility (DS-04/06/09), (4) share structure across the model population because models inherit brittleness from shared corpus statistics (DS-07/10), and (5) can therefore be inverted to infer training-data structure from behavior (DS-11) and monitored cheaply in training and deployment (BI series).

**Why it matters beyond NLP.** It replaces "can LLMs reason?" (unfalsifiable, exhausting) with "how much of this score is computation, how do we measure it, and what predicts when it fails" — a measurement science. That is the contribution: not a verdict on models, but the instrument and the laws a field needs to study them, the way psychometrics gave psychology the tools to study a mind it could not open.
