# DS-00 Deep Strategies — Overview

D01–D10 mostly live inside one paradigm: perturb input, observe output, infer strategy. The DS series changes the kind of science being done. Three families:

**Deeper measurement (DS-01, 02, 03, 05):** replace hand-made rules with fitted measurement models; read error content, not just error rate; measure representations, not just answers; test the model's knowledge of its own memory.
**Deeper causation (DS-04, 09):** manipulate the structure of exposure and of interference, not just its amount.
**Deeper theory (DS-06, 07, 08, 10, 11, 12):** move from cataloguing failures to predictive laws, population statistics, adaptive instruments, and inverse inference about training data.

Priority inside this series: DS-02 and DS-01 first (both ≈ $0, run on existing CSVs, and DS-02 feeds DS-01 as an indicator). DS-03 joins the D2 GPU sessions. DS-04 is the flagship causal experiment after D1-lite. Everything else is staged behind those.

Relationship to the main program: DS-01 upgrades [[EF-04_Convergence_Labels_MTMM]]; DS-02 adds a fourth signal to Probe 1; DS-03 extends [[D02_Causal_Patching]]; DS-04 extends [[D01_Controlled_Exposure_Validation]]; DS-05 rides on [[HP-06_D1Lite_Finetune_Calibration]]; DS-06 consumes [[D03_Continuous_Transfer_Distance]] outputs; DS-07 consumes existing cross-model data; DS-08 requires DS-01; DS-09/10/11 are new cheap probes; DS-12 is the long game.

Beginner path: read [[BM-00_Start_Here]] before attempting any of these.
