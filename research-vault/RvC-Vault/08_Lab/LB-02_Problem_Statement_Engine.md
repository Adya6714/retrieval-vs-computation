# LB-02 — Problem Statement Engine

A generator, not a list. Strong problem statements in this agenda all have the same skeleton:

> Benchmarks measure [performance on X]. That performance conflates [A] with [B]. We build an instrument that separates A from B **per instance**, and we validate the instrument against [ground truth or causal intervention].

## The generator (three dials)
**Dial 1 — construct (from the human sciences).** Pick a construct with decades of measurement literature: transfer distance (near/far, Barnett & Ceci), retrieval vs reconstruction (memory research), depth-of-processing, conceptual change vs accretion (science-education research), reliability as a trait (test-retest), proximal development (what's solvable with scaffolding vs without), taxonomy levels (remember/apply/analyze), metacognitive calibration.
**Dial 2 — measurement principle (from psychometrics/experimental method).** Convergent+discriminant validity (MTMM), criterion validity (calibrate against ground truth), IRT (separate item difficulty from subject ability), dose-response, test-retest reliability, causal mediation, pre-registration.
**Dial 3 — LLM-specific confound to kill.** Exposure/contamination, tokenization, format propensity, difficulty covariance, instruction-tuning artifacts, sampling temperature.
Turn all three dials → a problem statement. Your current program = (retrieval-vs-computation) × (convergent validity) × (exposure). Most combinations are unoccupied.

## Quality checklist (all five or it's weak)
1. Names a **construct**, not a dataset.
2. Contains a **falsifiable dissociation** (two hypotheses that predict different data).
3. Has a **validation path**: ground truth (D1-style), causal intervention (D2-style), or ecological corpus (D4-style).
4. Produces a **per-instance readout**, not only a leaderboard delta.
5. Names **who acts on the answer** (scientist / training engineer / deployer) and what they do differently.

## Anti-patterns (auto-reject)
"We show models fail at Y" (C1 accumulation). "A new benchmark for Z" with no validity argument. A mechanistic finding with no behavioral consequence. Any claim whose only evidence is agreement between correlated proxies.

## Eight worked statements (first four = current program, verbatim shape; last four = open, generated by the dials)
1. Same-score models conflate retrieval with computation → three orthogonal probes per instance → **validated by controlled exposure** (D1). [construct: solution strategy × principle: criterion validity × confound: exposure]
2. Rename fragility conflates lost computation with broken binding → **patch entity representations** and see which restores answers (D2). [binding × causal mediation × tokenization]
3. "Robustness" conflates cliff with slope → **graded distance ladders**, cliff location as model fingerprint (D3). [transfer distance × dose-response × difficulty]
4. Invariance-emergence conflates scale with accumulated exposure → **checkpoint sweeps with corpus-counted exposure** (D4). [development × longitudinal × contamination]
5. **Reliability as a trait:** is per-item fragility a stable property of (item, model) or sampling noise? Test-retest + parallel-forms reliability of the whole probe suite across reruns, temperatures, paraphrase forms. Nobody has published reliability coefficients for a reasoning diagnostic; without them no fragility claim is safe. [reliability × test-retest × sampling]
6. **Taxonomy-consistency probe:** for one concept, build a Bloom-style battery (recall the rule / apply it / analyze a violation / generate a new instance). Construct: does competence at one level predict the next, per concept? Humans show strong hierarchy; retrieval-heavy models should show *inverted or flat* profiles (apply without recall, or recall without transfer). [taxonomy levels × profile analysis × exposure]
7. **Conceptual change vs local patching:** teach a counter-fact or rule revision in-context or by light fine-tune; measure whether revision propagates to entailed instances (conceptual change) or only the taught surface (accretion). Bridges to the editing literature (C9) with a per-instance propagation readout. [conceptual change × generalization gradient × editing artifacts]
8. **Scaffolding sensitivity (proximal-development):** per item, the minimal scaffold (one hint, one worked example, one subgoal) that flips failure→success. Retrieval-consistent items should be scaffold-insensitive under rename (nothing to retrieve), computation-consistent items scaffold-responsive. Gives deployers an actionable per-task number: "hintable or not." [ZPD × titration × few-shot confound]

Statements 5–8 are candidate D11–D14; open a D-note only when one passes the checklist AND has an HP-able first experiment.
