Not another benchmark. A measurement science.

When two LLMs score the same on a benchmark, are they solving the same way — or is one computing and the other pattern-matching? Correct output does not prove correct process. That is the founding problem of neuropsychology and experimental psychology: inferring hidden architecture from observable behaviour in a mind you cannot open.

This programme builds a validated, convergent, multi-signal instrument (behavioural + mechanistic) that distinguishes retrieval-like from computation-like solving per instance, then uses it to establish capability claims, an architecture claim, and training-data laws.

Rename (W3) is one surface axis of the instrument — not the research motivation. The motivation is process diagnosis. Surface transforms, plan–execution isolation, injection, intrusion errors, causal patching, controlled-exposure fine-tunes, and developmental checkpoints are the toolkit.

      Method · Three probes

      Surface, process, proximity.

      Each probe carries an assumption the others do not share. A per-instance label is issued only on agreement — consistency is not yet validity; calibration against known exposure is the next gate.

      Probe 1 · Surface battery

      One problem → six answer-preserving forms. Metric: per-variant accuracy & W3 retention.

      Template. 
      Solve the following problem step by step.
Then give the final answer.

{problem_text}
      Metric. AccWv = correct / n; RW3 = AccW3 / Acccan where Acccan ≥ 0.30 (floor fixed before analysis).

      Assumption it cannot discharge alone. A W3 drop could be tokenization noise or residual difficulty. Cross-model opposite-direction fragility on the same items, plus length-matched nonces, bound that. Graded-distance ladders (D3) are the next refinement.

      Probe 2 · Plan vs execution

      Session A declares a plan; Session B executes without history. Optional mid-solve injection.

      CCI. Fraction of declared numeric steps matched in isolated execution (ε = 0.01). Hard cross-session isolation — unlike within-trace CoT faithfulness work, the execution session has no plan in context. Injection. At a sampled interior step, replace true intermediate with a plausible false value; score acceptance taxonomy and final-answer recovery (TEP).

      Scope. CCI is a fingerprint, not a pure score (parser coverage confounds). BW strict-PDDL aborts 84–100% of sessions: a protocol finding, not a model claim.

      Probe 3 · Corpus proximity

      n-gram windows over public corpus (Infini-gram). Within-family proximity proxy only.

      Metric. Infini-gram n-gram hit rate over public corpora by subtype (coin change ≫ shortest path ≫ WIS). Explicitly a proxy — not the training corpus of any closed model tested.

      What would establish it. Open-corpus models (OLMo + Dolma) where template counts are measurable in the actual training data (D4). Controlled fine-tunes where exposure is known by construction (D1-lite).

      Finding 1

      Numeric change is nearly free. Rename is not.

      Slope graph: canonical → W6 → W3 on arithmetic. W6 lines stay flat; W3 falls. Gemini .909 / .958 / .523. Source: probe1_per_model_variant.csv.

      What this establishes. A single “robustness” scalar summarises very little: the same model can be nearly free under numeric regeneration and fragile under rename on the identical algorithm. The load-bearing surface is the name frame, not the numerals. Source: vault EF-01; paper §Lexical and Numeric Robustness.

        Finding 5

        Robustness is not accuracy.

        Two probes that share no machinery agree when pooled — and neither tracks canonical accuracy.

        Point-biserial CCI × W3 correctness; cluster bootstrap. Acc vs retention ρ=.136 (p=.642). Source: P2_P1_convergence.csv.

        Power point. At typical per-family n the association is invisible (both null). Pooling reveals convergent validity (r=.243, p=.024). Discriminant: neither retention nor φ tracks canonical accuracy. This is why the programme treats robustness as a capacity, not a restatement of the leaderboard.

        Open mechanism (vault D06). Two testable accounts: goal states are more constrained (lower branching), or forward search from a sparse init is harder than backward chaining. Distinguishing them needs instances with matched init/goal complexity — a small, designed experiment under ~100 inference calls.

        Finding 6

        Compliance is not correctness.

        An injected false intermediate propagates — and the final answer still recovers.

        n=61 ALGO Phase-2B. Gemini rejects every injection (0% compliance). Source: injection_recovery_summary.csv.

        Standing hypothesis H5. Recovery under injection may mean the answer direction was fixed before the injected step. Patching at the injection position (D2) would decide that — currently behavioural evidence only. Intrusion errors on rename (o4-mini .619 on ALGO W3) are a separate retrieval fingerprint already scored on existing data.

        Methods appendix

        How the instrument is built.

        Bank composition

        GSM · 44 arithmetic word problems from GSM8K / GSM-Symbolic across proximity quartiles.

        BW · 65 Blocksworld instances (50 standard, 15 obfuscated) with PDDL encoding.

        ALGO · 110 DP problems: coin change, shortest path, weighted interval scheduling (51 near-duplicate families).

        Variant protocol

        W1 paraphrase · W2 structural reformat · W3 entity rename (vocabulary-disjoint, length-matched) · W4 formal notation · W5 direction reversal · W6 procedural regeneration (gold re-derived). Entity mapping persisted; verifier consumes it so renamed answers score correctly.

        Verifier design

        Semantic where possible: PDDL state simulator for planning; exact optimal solver for optimisation; numeric match for arithmetic. Gold submitted to own verifier before any model call; variants checked to differ as intended; failures excluded with reason.

        Probe prompt templates

        Zero-shot chain-of-thought at T=0 through a single gateway. CCI: plan session then isolated execution session (no shared history). Injection: identical to execution with one interior false state.

        Statistical treatment

        ALGO: 10,000-draw percentile cluster bootstrap over 51 near-duplicate families. GSM/BW: Wilson intervals. Retention floor Acccan ≥ 0.30 fixed before analysis. Kendall W against within-row rank permutation null (10,000 perms, seed 42).
