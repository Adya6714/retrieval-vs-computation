Retrieval vs computation · research programme

A measurement science for how language models solve problems.

Build the first validated, per-instance measurement science for how LLMs solve problems (retrieval vs computation), then use it to establish capability claims, an architecture claim, and training-data laws.

A correct answer is consistent with executing a procedure or with matching a seen pattern. Accuracy cannot separate them.

Group-level perturbation studies report that accuracy drops. They do not tell you, for a given instance, which process produced the answer.

Per-instance diagnosis is the object. Everything downstream — where the split lives in the network, what training conditions produce it, when it emerges — depends on first having an instrument that works at instance granularity.

Strategy catalogue

Inference machinery, not questionnaires.

The machine-psychology literature borrows tasks from cognitive science. This programme borrows the inference machinery — the methods those fields built to diagnose hidden process in minds they could not open.

Generative principle. For any method from a science of hidden minds, ask:
  1. What hidden property did it infer?
  2. What observable signature did it exploit?
  3. What is the LLM analogue of that signature?
  4. Does it give a per-instance, theory-grounded signal the instrument can use?

5b · The three probes

Each probe carries an assumption the others do not share. Labels issue only on agreement — consistency is not yet validity.

Probe 1 · Surface battery

One problem → six answer-preserving forms. Metric: AccWv; RW3 = AccW3/Acccan (floor Acccan ≥ .30).

Template.
Solve step by step. Give the final answer.

{problem_text}
Assumption alone cannot discharge: tokenization noise. Cross-model opposite-direction fragility + length-matched nonces bound it.

Probe 2 · Plan vs execution

Session A declares a plan; Session B executes with no history. Optional mid-solve injection.

CCI. Fraction of declared numeric steps matched (ε=0.01). Injection. Plausible false intermediate; score acceptance and final-answer recovery. CCI is a fingerprint — parser coverage confounds.

Probe 3 · Corpus proximity

Infini-gram n-gram windows. Within-family proxy only — not closed-model membership.

What establishes it: open-corpus models (OLMo+Dolma) and controlled fine-tunes where exposure is known by construction (D1-lite).

5c · Verification

Semantic where the domain allows: PDDL simulator (planning), exact optimal solver (optimisation), numeric match (arithmetic). Failures excluded with reason, never scored.

5d · Statistical treatment

Optimisation: 10,000-draw percentile cluster bootstrap over 51 near-duplicate families (110 items). GSM/BW: Wilson intervals. Retention floor Acccan ≥ 0.30 fixed before analysis. Kendall W against within-row rank permutation null (10,000 perms, seed 42).
